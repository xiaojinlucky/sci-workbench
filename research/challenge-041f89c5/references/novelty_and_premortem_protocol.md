# Three-Dimension Topic Alignment, Top-Venue Gap Ruler & Pre-Mortem Protocol

## 1. Three-Dimension Topic Viability Check
A viable topic aligns three independent dimensions simultaneously:
1. **Literature Context & Known Space**: Exact closest prior works and why their reported mechanisms/methods do not yet settle the target question.
2. **Logical & Empirical Gap**: A specific, falsifiable failure mode, unresolved conflict, or boundary condition — never "nobody studied this".
3. **Executable Feasibility**: Verified data, compute/experimental conditions, timeline, and team capability (unknowns marked `[BLOCKED_UNKNOWN]`, never zero friction).

## 2. Top-Venue Gap Ruler (Incremental vs. Transfer vs. Breakthrough)
- Compare the proposal against the strongest full-text neighbor in the corpus across: mechanistic depth, cohort/dataset representativeness, baseline fairness, and validation closure.
- Flag `scenario_transfer` honestly when an established algorithm/assay is moved to a new dataset/domain; do not inflate scenario transfer into `method_innovation`.

## 3. Pre-Mortem Risk Matrix (Mandatory Before Full Commitment)
Assume the project fails or is rejected at peer review, and trace:
1. Top failure modes (novelty scoop, missing baseline comparability, blocked prerequisite, opposing-evidence replication failure).
2. Earliest observable warning signal in a minimal pilot.
3. Preventive action before full spend.
4. Explicit stop or pivot trigger.
5. Scoped contingency plan.
