# Phased High-Fidelity Literature Synthesis Protocol

## 1. Epistemic Hedging Table
| Register | Access / Evidence Condition | Permitted Prose Phrasing | Forbidden Phrasing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `established_in_fulltext` | Inspected PDF/XML full text, no direct contradiction | "Full-text results in [ID] report...", "Across N full-text studies..." | "Globally proven", "universally settled" |
| `suggestive_abstract_only` | `abstract_only` source (methods/stats unread) | "Abstract metadata in [ID] suggests...", "Pending full-text verification..." | "Conclusively proved", "full methods confirm" |
| `contested_or_opposing` | Supportive + opposing/null findings coexist | "[ID-A] reports X under condition A, whereas [ID-B] observes null/opposing Y under condition B" | "Studies generally agree", "remains controversial" (without citing both sides) |
| `prospective_protocol_only` | Planned/proposed protocol or hypothesis | "Proposed protocol [ID] plans to evaluate..." | "Experimental results in [ID] demonstrated..." |
| `unverified_unknown` | Missing PDF, unreadable scan, or ungrounded quote | "Remains unverified (`UNKNOWN`) due to..." | Silent omission or fabrication |

## 2. Anti-Boilerplate & Information Fidelity Rules
- Organize sections around empirical themes discovered from the corpus, never forced generic headings.
- Compare *actual functional operations* (dataset/cohort, perturbation/catalyst/architecture, comparator, metric) rather than listing tool or paper names.
- Preserve key domain terms accurately and distinguish domain consensus, author-integrated interpretation, and unresolved conflict.
- Anchor every closing research gap to a specific missing comparison, unresolved conflict, or full-text access gap inside the stated scope.
